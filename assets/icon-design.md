# FormatKae 简约图标

2026-10-24 根据现有图标简化。用户要求：简约、无光泽，同时保留此前的无箭头、不规则轮廓要求。

以蓝色胶片轮廓、白色三条音频波形和透明镂空表达音视频主题，去掉原有的立体材质、高光、阴影与复杂层叠。
工具：内置 image_gen，使用原来的光泽版 `assets/app.png` 作为编辑目标。

最终资源为 `assets/app.png`，保留生成结果原始透明通道；`assets/app.ico` 仅作尺寸和文件格式转换，包含 16、20、24、32、40、48、64、96、128、256 像素版本。
同一 ICO 用于 Windows EXE、窗口、应用默认图标与侧栏；打包资源为 `_internal/assets/app.ico`。

## 最终编辑提示词

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: minimalist Windows desktop application icon. Input image 1 is the existing FormatKae icon to edit. Simplify this SAME icon into a radically cleaner, flat two-dimensional graphic. Preserve its identity: an asymmetric irregular outer silhouette, a simplified curved film strip integrated with three rounded audio equalizer bars, the existing blue-violet color family, transparent background, no arrows. Replace the complex folded ribbons with ONE broad clean flat film ribbon with only a few large rectangular perforations and three simple white vertical rounded bars. Use ONLY TWO solid opaque colors: cobalt/periwinkle blue #527FF5 for the main silhouette and white #FFFFFF for the three equalizer bars; use transparent cutouts for the perforations. Reduce folds, remove small details and overlapping layers, use broad geometric masses, clear negative space, and a bold readable icon at 24 pixels. Make the outer edge visibly irregular and asymmetric, with a few smooth lobes rather than a square, circle or rounded-square tile. Absolutely FLAT solid color fills: NO gradients, gloss, sheen, lighting, highlights, reflections, metallic or glass effects, shine, bloom, bevel, emboss, 3D, shadows, textures, outlines or decorative particles. No arrow, arrowhead, chevron, play triangle, text, letters or watermark. Single centered icon only, not a mockup. Preserve genuine transparency outside the icon and in its cutouts, with roughly 10 percent transparent margin on all sides.
